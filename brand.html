Brixton Forged TR04 Aero+

Brand embed (code 614). One wheel, a few finishes. The visitor uploads a photo and picks a finish.

What to try

  1. Open the button, upload test-car.jpg (in this folder) or your own car photo.
  2. Pick a finish at the bottom, press Render.
  3. Press Request quote. It goes to a test inbox, so use any name and email.
  4. Keep rendering until you reach the limit (5 per device).

Every render is a real render. This is our own test brand, so it is never refused for billing. This page talks to staging, never to the live site.
