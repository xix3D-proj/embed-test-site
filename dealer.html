See any wheel on your car

Dealer embed (code 616). Visitors pick from the shop's whole catalogue: about 320 brands.

What to try

  1. Open the button, upload test-car.jpg (in this folder) or your own car photo.
  2. Pick a brand, search a wheel, pick it and a finish, press Render.
  3. Press More wheels, pick a wheel from another brand, render again.
  4. Press Request quote. It goes to a test inbox, so use any name and email.
  5. Keep rendering until you reach the limit (5 per device).

Every render is a real render and uses a real credit on the test shop. This page talks to staging, never to the live site.
